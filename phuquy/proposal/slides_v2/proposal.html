SFT learns from a reference solution, whereas GRPO learns only from solutions sampled from the model.

The 8 sampled solutions (rollouts) form a group. The pass rate $p$ is the fraction of correct solutions in the group.

SFT: supervised fine-tuning. RL: reinforcement learning. GRPO: Group Relative Policy Optimization, the RL method used in all experiments.

Notation

SymbolMeaning
$x,\ y^*$question and reference solution
$r(x,y)$1 if the final answer of solution $y$ is correct, otherwise 0
$\pi_\theta$model being trained
$\mu$source of guided tokens: $y^*$ or samples from $\pi_\theta$
$q_t$target distribution at token $t$, derived from $y^*$
$D$per-token divergence between $\pi_\theta$ and $q_t$, e.g. KL
$\lambda,\ w_t$guidance weight per step ($\lambda$) and per token ($w_t$)

8

A general objective

$$\max_{\theta}\;\underbrace{\colorbox{#dde8dc}{$\mathbb{E}_{y\sim\pi_\theta}\big[\,r(x,y)\,\big]$}}_{J_{\mathrm{RL}}(\theta)\text{: expected reward}}\;-\;\underbrace{\colorbox{#efe3cc}{$\lambda\,\mathbb{E}_{y\sim\mu}\sum_{t} w_t\, D\big(\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid x,y_{<t}),\,q_t\big)$}}_{L_{\mathrm{guide}}(\theta)\text{: guidance from }y^*}$$

Each combination method corresponds to a choice of $\mu$, $q$, $D$, $\lambda$ and $w$.

$y_{<t}$: the first $t-1$ tokens of $y$. Both expectations are also taken over training questions $x$.
